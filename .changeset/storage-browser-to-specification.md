The storage browser treats `.genoacms/` in the default bucket as read-only for users, takes typed names as one level, renames and moves only what was chosen, answers denials with 403, reports actions on several items per item, shows long directories a page at a time with `Show more`, keeps the selection across reloads, and names the picker's confirm button `Confirm selection`.
